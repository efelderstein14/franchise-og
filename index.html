Questions

What people ask me before they book.

Is the call really free?

Yes. No cost and no obligation. It is a conversation, not a sales call.

I am just starting to look. Is it too early?

No. Most people I talk to are early. That is the best time to call, before you sign with anyone or spend money on anything.

Do I need franchise or business experience?

No. Most franchise owners have never run one before, and many good brokers came from corporate sales, HR or consulting. What you bring matters more than what you know today.

Will you push me toward a particular franchise?

No. I will explain exactly how brokers get paid so you know where everyone stands. If franchising is wrong for you right now, I will tell you.

What should I bring?

Your background, what draws you to franchising, and any brands or broker networks you are already looking at. That is plenty.
