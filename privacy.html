Who we are

The Franchise OG website is operated by MFO Inc. (“we”, “us”, “our”). This policy explains what information we collect through the website and the free intro call, how we use and share it, and the choices you have.

Questions about this policy or your information: topfranchises@gmail.com.

Information we collect

Information you give us. When you request a call, we collect your name, phone number, email address, and what you tell us about your background and your interest in franchise brokering. We also keep notes from our conversations with you.

Information collected automatically. When you visit the site, we and our partners collect technical information such as your browser and device type, IP address and approximate location, the pages you view, the site that referred you, and how you interact with the page. We collect this using cookies, pixels and similar technologies from analytics and advertising providers.

How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • respond to your request and call you;
  • prepare for and follow up on our conversation;
  • send you emails and text messages about franchise brokering and related opportunities, where the law allows and, for texts, with your consent;
  • measure and improve the website and our advertising, including showing you our ads on social media and other sites;
  • protect the site, prevent misuse, and comply with the law.

How we share your information

We share your information with:

  • Our affiliates, meaning companies that control, are controlled by, or are under common control with MFO Inc. They may use your information for the purposes in this policy, including contacting you about franchise brokering opportunities.
  • Service providers that work on our behalf, such as our form, email, text messaging, hosting and analytics providers.
  • Advertising and social media partners, such as Meta, LinkedIn and Google, through the pixels and cookies on the site. They use this information to measure our ads and to show ads to you and to people like you.
  • Others when required: to comply with the law or legal process, to protect rights and safety, or as part of a merger, sale or other transfer of our business.

We do not sell your information for money. Some state laws treat sharing information with advertising partners for targeted ads as a “sale” or “sharing”. You can opt out of this at any time; see Your choices and rights.

Cookies, analytics and ad pixels

We use analytics tools to understand how people use the site, and advertising pixels to measure and target our ads. You can limit this by:

  • blocking or deleting cookies in your browser settings;
  • using the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on;
  • adjusting your ad preferences in your Meta, LinkedIn and Google account settings;
  • emailing topfranchises@gmail.com to opt out of targeted advertising.

Some parts of the site may not work as intended if you block cookies.

Emails and text messages

You can unsubscribe from marketing emails using the link in any email. You can stop text messages at any time by replying STOP. Message and data rates may apply, and message frequency varies.

Agreeing to receive marketing texts is never a condition of requesting a call. Even after you opt out of marketing, we may still contact you about a call you requested.

How long we keep information

We keep your information for as long as we need it for the purposes in this policy, for example while we are in touch about franchise brokering, and as required for legal, tax or record-keeping reasons. Then we delete it or de-identify it.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect your information. No website or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • know what personal information we have about you and get a copy;
  • correct information that is wrong;
  • ask us to delete your information;
  • opt out of marketing, and of the sale or sharing of your information for targeted advertising.

To make a request, email topfranchises@gmail.com. We will verify your request before acting on it, and we will not treat you differently for exercising your rights. An authorized agent may make a request for you with your written permission.

Children

The site and the call are meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

Visitors outside the United States

We are based in the United States, and your information is processed and stored there. By using the site, you understand that your information will be handled under U.S. law.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed. If we make significant changes, we will post a notice on the site.
